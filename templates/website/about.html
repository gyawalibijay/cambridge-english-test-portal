About the platform

A focused English practice environment built around the learner journey.

Arya Academy brings test access, learning resources, timed practice, recorded responses and progress reporting together in one portal.

What we are building

One system from preparation to results

Students should not need one website for courses, another for payments, another for test practice and another for feedback. The portal is designed to keep those activities connected to the same account.

For administrators

Content and access can be managed centrally

Administrators can manage licensed programs, student access, purchases, courses, learning materials, test content, question-bank workflows and evaluations.

Student-first UI Clear navigation, dedicated test runners and result history.
Program-based access Students purchase only the test system they need.
Independent practice Practice feedback is clearly distinguished from official examination scoring.
