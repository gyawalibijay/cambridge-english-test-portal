FAQ

Everything needed for a client-ready draft

Can students practise sections separately?

Yes. The student dashboard links directly to each of the four practice skills.

Does the portal save student progress?

Attempts, submitted responses and available feedback remain attached to the signed-in student account.

Is there a dark mode?

Yes. The public website, sign-in, registration, profile and dashboard use a persistent light/dark theme preference.

Is this an official Cambridge website?

No. It is an independent practice portal. Portal-authored material should remain labelled as practice content rather than official Cambridge examination material.
