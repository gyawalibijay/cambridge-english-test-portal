How it works

Know exactly what happens after you create an account.

The portal keeps the purchase, learning, practice and result flow simple and visible.

Create your profile

Register once with your student details and sign in to your personal dashboard.

Choose test access

Select the program package you need and submit your payment details for verification.

Use courses and materials

Published resources for your purchased program become available inside the course library.

Practise and review

Complete test sections, save responses and review your practice reports and progress.

Speaking

Record → replay → submit → review

Speaking uses microphone recording, timing and replay. Audio-quality checks prevent silent or unusable recordings from receiving misleading practice credit.

Reading & Listening

Answer → submit → objective marking

Where structured answer keys exist, objective responses can be marked automatically and shown in the student's results.

Writing

Write → autosave → submit → feedback

Writing uses focused task screens, live word count and a workflow that can support automated practice feedback and evaluator review.

Results

History → report → next practice

Students can revisit attempts and use the report to decide which skill or course resource to work on next.
