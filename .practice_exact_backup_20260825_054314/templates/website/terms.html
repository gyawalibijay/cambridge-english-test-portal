Platform terms of use

A concise draft for the current development portal.

Practice platform

This portal provides English preparation, learning resources and practice assessment tools. It does not represent an official examination provider unless a separate written arrangement expressly states otherwise.

Practice scores

Automated and AI-assisted scores, feedback and CEFR-style estimates are for practice and should not be treated as official examination results or guarantees of future performance.

Accounts and access

Users are responsible for the accuracy of information submitted through their account and for keeping login credentials secure. Paid program access is subject to the package and verification status shown in the portal.

Uploaded materials

Administrators should upload only content they are authorized to use and should accurately identify source or ownership information where appropriate.

Changes

Features, package details and these draft terms may be updated as the platform moves from development to production.
